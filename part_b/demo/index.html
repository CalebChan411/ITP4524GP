How does this demo relate to the course?

Content-based filtering builds a preference vector from liked-game tags and uses cosine similarity to score candidates. User-based collaborative filtering finds players with shared favourites and combines their weighted support. The popularity baseline ranks games by example likes. These illustrate Lecture 9; their scores have different meanings and are not probabilities.

The formal project will use real, cited data and a defined training/test split to compare methods. This demo reports no accuracy or test-set performance.
